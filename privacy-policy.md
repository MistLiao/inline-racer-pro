# Inline Racer Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16

Inline Racer Pro ("the game") is made by the Inline Racer Pro team ("we", "us").
This policy explains what information the iOS and Android versions of the game handle. Questions: inlineracerpro@gmail.com.

## Summary

- The game has no accounts and we run no servers. We do not receive your personal data.
- Your progress and settings stay on your device.
- On iOS and Android, the game can show **optional rewarded ads** from Google AdMob when
  you choose "Watch ad" for an extra training attempt. Google collects data to serve
  those ads, as described below.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings (audio, language, controls, display), progress (abilities,
training attempts, unlocked venues, cup progress) and race records on your device only.
It is not sent to us. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud
or Google backup) according to your operating system settings. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Advertising (iOS and Android)

The mobile versions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging
Platform (UMP). Ads are only shown when you tap "Watch ad"; races never show ads.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking; on Android the
  Android Advertising ID),
- IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
- ad interactions (ads requested, shown, clicked) and diagnostic data from the SDK.

This is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalise
ads. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps
that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The list
of Google's ad technology partners is shown in the consent message.

### Your choices

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are
  shown. You can consent, decline, or choose by purpose and partner. Change your choice at
  any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options**. Some partners may rely on legitimate
  interest; you can object in the same screen. If you decline, ads that are shown are
  non-personalised or limited.
- **United States:** if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example
  California, Colorado or Connecticut), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your
  personal information for ads at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options** by choosing
  "Don't sell or share my data".
- **iOS:** the game asks for permission to track only if Google's consent flow requires it.
  You can change it in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **Android:** reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**
  (wording varies by device).
- Ads are optional: you can always wait for the free daily training attempts instead.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not
knowingly allow ads to be personalised for children.

## Data retention and your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so we have nothing to access, correct or delete.
Data collected by Google for advertising is retained and handled under Google's policies;
see https://policies.google.com/privacy for how to exercise your rights with Google.
For any other privacy question, contact inlineracerpro@gmail.com.

## Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and revise the effective
date above.
