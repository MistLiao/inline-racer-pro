# Inline Racer Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Publisher: MistLiao · Contact: inlineracerpro@gmail.com

## Scope and contact

This policy describes how MistLiao handles data for Inline Racer Pro on iOS/iPadOS and Android, and this support website. The app is being prepared for release. This policy will be reviewed against the final build and updated before release if its practices differ.

For privacy questions, access/correction or deletion requests, use the contact email below. Please provide only enough information to identify your request; do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification method has first been explained.

## Local game data

The game saves your chosen skater name, appearance, body type, suit, abilities, training progress, settings, race records, cup progress and unlocks on your device. Personal-best ghosts save in-game character motion, positions and checkpoint splits for later comparison. These are virtual game data, not your physical location or fitness measurements.

The local device date and remaining attempts manage daily training limits. The current game has no developer account system or developer-operated cloud save service that receives these saves. Local data remains in the app container until changed or removed; system backups and restore settings may affect how long copies remain. Support cannot retrieve local saves from a developer server.

You can edit the settings and skater details exposed by the game. Removing the app may remove local progress; offloading and restoring backups can behave differently. Contact support before removing it if you want help preserving progress. Do not assume reinstalling restores saves.

## Ads, permissions and platform services

The iOS/iPadOS and Android apps use Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform to provide optional rewarded training ads. Ads may generate revenue. Google services may receive data for consent management, ad requests and ad delivery, as described below. We do not use support messages for advertising, sell them, or share them with data brokers.

The mobile game path disables desktop camera/head-tracking features. In-game training is a virtual mechanic, not medical advice or a physical activity measurement. Apple and Google may handle backups, store transactions or system diagnostics under your system settings and its own policy. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers through Apple, available crash/diagnostic reports may be used to investigate stability issues.

## Advertising data and choices

Depending on SDK operation, permissions and consent, Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad and product interactions, crash logs, performance and other diagnostic data for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and consent management. Local skater names, saves and ghosts are not intentionally passed to the ad SDK by our game scripts.

At startup, the app contacts Google to update consent status and shows a consent form when required and available. When that status permits ad requests, the ad SDK initializes and can preload a rewarded ad before you choose to watch one. Settings exposes Advertising privacy options when the consent service requires it. Apple tracking permission may be requested when applicable. Declining tracking does not prevent every network request or all non-tracking data processing. If consent or ads are unavailable, you can continue racing and use the next day's free training attempts.

For the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the consent message provides choices about ad purposes and partners when applicable. Where applicable US state privacy messages are required, Advertising privacy options provides the sale or sharing opt-out. Use Settings to reopen those choices when the consent service requires this entry. On iOS, system tracking choices are also available in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system privacy/ads settings; wording varies by device. Optional ads are not required to race.

Google may process data outside your country under its policies. Google controls retention for its advertising and consent services; our 12-month support retention limit does not apply to those services. See Google's privacy policy and advertising controls for access, deletion and ad choices. Before public store release, the final consent behavior and store privacy disclosures will be reviewed again.

## Support messages and retention

If you email support, we receive your email address, display name, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate issues and follow up on the same problem. Support uses Gmail (Google); email delivery, storage and spam filtering may process data outside your country under Google's policies.

We retain support correspondence and attachments for up to 12 months after the issue is closed, then delete them, unless retention is required by law. You can request earlier deletion by email. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request concerns your correspondence. Provider backups may take additional time to expire under their retention processes; we do not promise immediate erasure from all backups.

Only the publisher and service providers needed to operate support process these messages, except when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment details.

## This website

The hosting layer can set necessary security cookies, including Cloudflare's __cf_bm to distinguish automated traffic. Cloudflare documents a 30-minute inactivity expiry for that cookie. Other security measures may apply under its cookie policy. This hosting behavior is separate from the static page source.

These static pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, forms, cookies or browser storage. Opening the page still sends ordinary request information (such as IP address, browser information and time) to hosting/delivery providers to serve and secure it. The support site uses OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure; a copy of this policy is also hosted on GitHub Pages. The providers process requests under their applicable policies. Following the email link opens your mail app; sending a message is your choice.

## Children and changes

The app has not been designated for the Kids Category. We do not ask children to send personal information to support. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's message or a deletion request. The store age rating will reflect the final content review and questionnaire.

Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. A change to SDKs, advertising or off-device processing requires a new review before release.
